# Before and after: a product update email

## Before

> Here's the thing: we didn't just ship a feature this week. We shipped a paradigm shift. Our new scheduling tool leverages cutting-edge automation to streamline your workflow, underscoring our commitment to empowering teams. It's not about saving time. It's about reclaiming it. At the end of the day, this changes everything.

## After

> We shipped scheduling this week. It books a meeting across three calendars in one click, which took our own team about four minutes by hand. Try it from the Calendar tab and tell me what breaks.

## What changed

- Cut the throat-clearing opener and the binary contrasts; the point now opens the email.
- Replaced "leverages cutting-edge automation to streamline your workflow" with what the feature does and a measured before.
- Removed "paradigm shift," "empowering," "at the end of the day," "this changes everything."
- Ended on a concrete ask instead of a kicker.
- Kept the writer's direct, first-person tone and the "tell me what breaks" invitation, which was the one human line in the original.
